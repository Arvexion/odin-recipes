The Most Best Donut

Home

Description

Baked chocolate donuts feature a rich, cocoa-infused cake batter made by mixing flour, cocoa powder, sugar, eggs, and buttermilk. This thick batter is piped into a donut pan and baked at 350°F (177°C) for about 10 minutes, resulting in a soft, fudgy texture.

Once cooled, the donuts are dipped into a simple glaze of whisked powdered sugar, milk, and vanilla. The glaze quickly dries into a sweet, crackly shell that perfectly balances the deep chocolate flavor.

Ingredients

  • All-purpose flour: 1 cup
  • Unsweetened cocoa powder: ⅓ cup
  • Granulated sugar: ½ cup
  • Baking powder: 1 tsp/li>
  • Baking soda: ¼ tsp
  • Salt: ¼ tsp
  • Buttermilk: ½ cup (keeps the donut moist)
  • Egg: 1 large (at room temperature)
  • Melted butter or vegetable oil: 2 tbsp
  • Vanilla extract: 1 tsp
  • Powdered sugar: 1 ½ cups
  • Milk: 3 to 4 tbsp (adjust for your preferred thickness)
  • Vanilla extract: 1 tsp

Steps

  1. Preheat your oven to 350°F (177°C) and lightly grease a 6-cavity donut pan.
  2. Whisk the flour, cocoa powder, granulated sugar, baking powder, baking soda, and salt together in a large bowl.
  3. Whisk the buttermilk, egg, melted butter, and vanilla extract together in a separate bowl.
  4. Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients and stir gently just until the flour disappears (do not overmix).
  5. Spoon or pipe the thick batter into the donut pan cavities, filling them about two-thirds full.
  6. Bake for 8 to 10 minutes until the tops spring back when touched, then transfer them to a wire rack to cool completely.
  7. Whisk the powdered sugar, milk, and vanilla together in a shallow bowl until smooth.
  8. Dip the top of each cooled donut into the glaze, let the excess drip off, and place them back on the rack for 5 minutes to set.

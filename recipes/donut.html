Description

Baked chocolate donuts feature a rich, cocoa-infused cake batter made by mixing flour, cocoa powder, sugar, eggs, and buttermilk. This thick batter is piped into a donut pan and baked at 350°F (177°C) for about 10 minutes, resulting in a soft, fudgy texture.

Once cooled, the donuts are dipped into a simple glaze of whisked powdered sugar, milk, and vanilla. The glaze quickly dries into a sweet, crackly shell that perfectly balances the deep chocolate flavor.

Ingredients

  • All-purpose flour: 1 cup
  • Unsweetened cocoa powder: ⅓ cup
  • Granulated sugar: ½ cup
  • Baking powder: 1 tsp/li>
  • Baking soda: ¼ tsp
  • Salt: ¼ tsp
  • Buttermilk: ½ cup (keeps the donut moist)
  • Egg: 1 large (at room temperature)
  • Melted butter or vegetable oil: 2 tbsp
  • Vanilla extract: 1 tsp
  • Powdered sugar: 1 ½ cups
  • Milk: 3 to 4 tbsp (adjust for your preferred thickness)
  • Vanilla extract: 1 tsp

Description

Traditional chocolate ice cream is made by crafting a custard base using milk, heavy cream, sugar, and egg yolks, which are gently cooked until thick. Unsweetened cocoa powder or melted dark chocolate is whisked into this warm mixture to provide a deep flavor, which is then thoroughly chilled before being poured into an ice cream maker to churn and freeze.

For an incredibly easy shortcut, a no-churn recipe completely skips the eggs, cooking, and churning machine by using whipped cream instead. You simply fold a mixture of cocoa powder and sweetened condensed milk into stiffly whipped heavy cream, which locks in thousands of tiny air bubbles to keep the ice cream soft and scoopable once frozen.

Ingredients

  • Heavy whipping cream: 2 cups (cold)
  • Sweetened condensed milk: 1 can (14 ounces, chilled)
  • Unsweetened cocoa powder: ½ cup
  • Vanilla extract: 1 teaspoon
  • Fine sea salt: A tiny pinch
